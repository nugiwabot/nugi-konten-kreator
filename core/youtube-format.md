# Nugi YouTube Format — Production Layer

## 1. Purpose

This document is the source of truth for the **YouTube production format** of Nugi.

It defines how Nugi's existing editorial/story system is packaged into a recognizable YouTube video without replacing the existing editorial DNA.

The core editorial identity remains:

> **HUMAN × PLACE × CHANGE × WHY**

The Story Engine remains the source of truth for the narrative itself.

This document adds the **YouTube production layer** on top of that story.

---

## 2. Architecture

A Nugi YouTube video has three conceptual layers:

```
NUGI YOUTUBE VIDEO
│
├── CHANNEL IDENTITY
│   ├── Cold Open
│   ├── Micro Bumper
│   ├── Sonic / Visual Signature
│   ├── Optional Recurring Phrase
│   └── Closing Identity
│
├── STORY
│   ├── Observation
│   ├── Question
│   ├── Narrative Device
│   ├── Evidence
│   ├── Context
│   ├── Deeper WHY
│   ├── Revelation
│   └── Reflection / Open Question
│
└── YOUTUBE PACKAGING
    ├── Chapters
    ├── Source Cards / On-screen Evidence
    ├── Optional Soft CTA
    ├── End Screen
    ├── Description Metadata
    └── Thumbnail Concept
```

These are **conceptual layers**, not required physical folders.

Do not create a separate `NUGI VIDEO/` directory for them.

---

## 3. Non-Negotiable Editorial Principle

### Story comes first.

Channel branding and YouTube conventions must never overpower the story.

The default priority is:

1. Human relevance
2. Story clarity
3. Curiosity
4. Evidence
5. Revelation
6. Reflection
7. Channel identity
8. Packaging

A YouTube convention must be omitted or shortened when it damages the narrative.

Nugi is not a generic YouTube-template channel.

---

# 4. CHANNEL IDENTITY

## 4.1 Cold Open

Every long-form video should consider a **cold open** before the main explanatory setup.

Purpose:

- create immediate curiosity
- reveal the central tension/question
- establish the human consequence
- give the audience a reason to continue

The cold open should normally be:

- concrete
- visual
- human
- specific
- concise

Avoid generic openings such as:

- "Halo guys, balik lagi..."
- long self-introductions
- channel announcements
- generic motivational statements
- long explanations before the central question appears

The cold open should lead naturally into the story.

### Conceptual flow

```
COLD OPEN
    ↓
CENTRAL QUESTION / PROMISE
    ↓
MICRO BRANDING
    ↓
STORY
```

The exact wording and duration are story-dependent.

---

## 4.2 Micro Bumper

Nugi may use a very short visual/audio bumper after the cold open.

Purpose:

- reinforce channel recognition
- create a small breathing point
- mark the transition from hook to investigation

The bumper should be **micro**, not a long intro sequence.

Do not use a long logo animation or a long verbal introduction before the story.

---

## 4.3 Sonic / Visual Signature

Nugi should gradually develop a recognizable visual and sonic language.

Possible components:

- short audio sting
- restrained logo treatment
- recurring typography
- consistent source-card treatment
- consistent map / timeline / evidence visual language
- recurring transitions

The signature should feel documentary / essay-like rather than promotional.

It should support the story instead of interrupting it.

---

## 4.4 Recurring Phrase

A recurring Nugi phrase is optional.

It should emerge naturally from the channel's existing philosophy rather than becoming a forced slogan.

Possible direction:

> "Kelihatannya biasa. Tapi kenapa bisa begini?"

or:

> "Kita bukan cuma mencari apa yang terjadi. Kita mencari kenapa."

These are **directional examples, not finalized mandatory slogans**.

Do not repeat a catchphrase mechanically in every video.

---

## 4.5 Closing Identity

After the editorial reflection and open question, Nugi may use a short channel closing.

Purpose:

- reinforce the channel identity
- signal the end of the investigation
- transition naturally into the end screen

The closing should be brief and calm.

Avoid turning the ending into a long promotional segment.

---

# 5. STORY LAYER

The Story Engine remains unchanged.

The selected **Story Type** determines the narrative structure.

Examples:

### HUMAN_DILEMMA

```
Situation
→ Choice A
→ Choice B
→ Trade-off
→ Systemic Cause
→ Human Consequence
→ Revelation
→ Reflection
```

### HIDDEN_SYSTEM

```
Observation
→ Question
→ Surface
→ Hidden Mechanism
→ Evidence
→ Consequences
→ Revelation
→ Reflection
```

### SECOND_ORDER

```
Initial Change
→ First-Order Effect
→ Question
→ Second-Order Effect
→ Evidence
→ Revelation
→ Reflection
```

### PLACE

```
Observation of Place
→ Question
→ Spatial Context
→ Historical / Structural Cause
→ Evidence
→ Revelation
→ Reflection
```

Other Story Types continue to follow the existing Story Engine definitions.

### Important rule

Do not force one universal YouTube story structure onto every Nugi video.

Story Type remains the source of truth.

Contradiction remains optional unless the selected Story Type is `CONTRADICTION`.

---

# 6. RE-HOOK / RETENTION LAYER

Long-form videos may use **re-hooks** when the story reaches a natural transition point.

Purpose:

- restore attention
- introduce the next layer of the investigation
- reopen the central question
- prevent a long uninterrupted explanatory block

Examples of re-hook functions:

- "Tapi ternyata masalahnya bukan cuma itu."
- "Dan di sinilah ceritanya mulai berubah."
- "Tapi ada satu biaya yang belum kita hitung."
- "Kalau itu benar, muncul pertanyaan yang lebih aneh."

These are functional examples, not mandatory wording.

### Rule

Do not impose a fixed re-hook interval such as every 2 minutes.

Use a re-hook only when the narrative benefits from one.

---

# 7. REVELATION AND REFLECTION

The existing Nugi editorial DNA remains:

```
Observation
→ Question
→ Evidence
→ Context
→ Deeper WHY
→ Revelation
→ Reflection
```

The **Revelation** is the intellectual peak.

It should:

- explain a meaningful causal relationship
- connect the system to ordinary human life
- provide a new way to see the original phenomenon

The **Reflection / Open Question** should:

- avoid a forced moral conclusion
- avoid motivational clichés
- leave room for the audience to think independently
- connect the insight back to human life

# 8. YOUTUBE PACKAGING

## 8.1 Chapters

Long-form videos should have YouTube-ready chapter candidates.

Internal Stage labels are not automatically the final public chapter titles.

Example:

```
INTERNAL:
STAGE 3 — SISTEM YANG TERSEMBUNYI

PUBLIC CHAPTER:
06:20 — Sistem yang Tidak Kita Sadari
```

Chapter titles should be understandable to viewers and should reflect actual content.

---

## 8.2 Source Cards / On-Screen Evidence

Important factual claims should be convertible into visual evidence.

A source cue may contain:

```
[SOURCE CARD]
Institution / Publication

[ON SCREEN]
What should appear visually

[VISUAL]
Chart / map / document / archival image

[NARRATION]
Relevant spoken claim
```

The purpose is not to display citations constantly.

The purpose is to make important evidence understandable and traceable during the video.

---

## 8.3 Fact / Interpretation / Scenario

Nugi scripts should distinguish three levels when relevant:

### FACT

What the evidence directly establishes.

### INTERPRETATION

A reasoned explanation or synthesis of the evidence.

### SCENARIO

A possible future or downstream outcome based on stated assumptions.

Do not present interpretation or scenario as established fact.

This is especially important for:

- AI
- future cities
- economics
- housing markets
- historical causality
- second-order effects

---

## 8.4 Soft CTA

CTA is optional.

When used, it should come after the editorial value has already been delivered.

Preferred characteristics:

- short
- relevant
- calm
- non-manipulative

Avoid stacking:

- like
- subscribe
- comment
- share
- notification
- link in bio

into a long closing speech.

The story should remain the main product.

---

## 8.5 End Screen

The end screen belongs to the YouTube packaging layer rather than the editorial story.

It may direct viewers toward:

- another relevant Nugi video
- a related topic
- channel subscription

The end screen should not force an unrelated recommendation.

---

## 8.6 Description Metadata

Production-ready scripts should eventually support a description package containing:

- short synopsis
- key topics
- chapter list
- primary sources
- notable evidence references
- optional related-video references

This layer is packaging, not part of the spoken narration.

---

## 8.7 Thumbnail Concept

Each long-form script should support a thumbnail concept based on:

- one dominant idea
- one visual tension
- very limited text
- clear emotional / intellectual promise

Do not turn the thumbnail concept into clickbait that the video cannot support.

---

# 9. CURRENT LONG-FORM PRODUCTION PROFILE

The current Nugi long-form production set in `output/` is designed around approximately **16–20 minutes** per video.

This is the current working production profile for the present batch, not a requirement that every future Nugi video must have the same duration.

Duration should ultimately follow the strength of the story.

Do not pad a story to reach a target duration.

---

# 10. Recommended Long-Form Flow

A typical Nugi long-form video may look like:

```
COLD OPEN
↓
CENTRAL QUESTION / PROMISE
↓
MICRO BUMPER
↓
STORY TYPE-DRIVEN NARRATIVE
↓
RE-HOOK (when useful)
↓
DEEPER WHY
↓
EVIDENCE / SOURCE VISUALS
↓
REVELATION
↓
REFLECTION / OPEN QUESTION
↓
CLOSING IDENTITY
↓
OPTIONAL SOFT CTA
↓
END SCREEN
```

This is a **production wrapper**, not a replacement for the Story Engine.

---

# 11. What Nugi YouTube Is NOT

Do not turn Nugi into:

- a generic "YouTube guru" channel
- a personality-first vlog
- a sales presentation
- a reaction channel
- a listicle template factory
- an aggressive CTA machine
- a channel that uses long logo intros
- a channel that forces contradiction into every story

The Nugi promise remains:

> **"Nugi membongkar hal-hal yang kelihatannya biasa, tetapi ternyata menentukan cara kita hidup."**

---

# 12. Phase Boundaries

This document defines the YouTube format only.

It does NOT authorize:

- changes to the Python engine
- changes to the media pipeline
- changes to the research engine
- creation of a new `NUGI VIDEO/` directory
- forced regeneration of existing scripts
- rewriting the five existing long-form scripts automatically

Those are separate phases.

---

# 13. Future Production Contract

When `skills/generate-script.md` is updated in the next phase, it should:

1. Keep Story Type as the story source of truth.
2. Apply this YouTube format as a production wrapper.
3. Generate channel identity elements only when appropriate.
4. Support re-hooks without fixed timing.
5. Produce YouTube-ready chapters.
6. Produce source/on-screen evidence cues when useful.
7. Distinguish fact, interpretation, and scenario where relevant.
8. Keep CTA optional and soft.
9. Preserve Nugi's non-sales, reflective tone.
10. Avoid generic YouTube intro and outro templates.

---

## 14. Success Criteria

A Nugi YouTube script is format-complete when:

- the story can stand on its own without branding
- the opening creates immediate curiosity
- channel identity is recognizable but brief
- Story Type still determines the narrative
- evidence can become on-screen material
- important claims can be sourced visibly
- re-hooks appear only when useful
- the ending contains reflection rather than forced moralizing
- CTA remains optional
- chapters can be generated
- packaging can be handed to an editor
- the result feels like **Nugi**, not a generic YouTube template
